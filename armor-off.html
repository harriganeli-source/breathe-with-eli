Armor Off

A Half-Day Workshop

with Jesse Torgerson & Eli Harrigan

Saturday, April 19th • 1pm–6pm • Victor, NY

Register Now

Most of us are walking around in armor we didn't consciously choose.

It went on piece by piece — in the moments when being open cost us something. A sharp word. A relationship that ended badly. Years of holding it together at work, at home, in the world. After a while, bracing became so automatic we stopped noticing we were doing it.

The armor keeps out pain. It also keeps out everything else.

This workshop is about taking it off.

What This Actually Is

Not a talk. Not a seminar. Not an afternoon of affirmations.

This is physical work. We use the breath, the body, and the voice to get underneath the surface — the places where old tension and unexpressed energy have been sitting so long you've started calling them personality traits.

It isn't always comfortable. But most people walk out feeling something they didn't expect: lighter. Like they put something down they'd been carrying for years without realizing it.

What We'll Do

Dynamic Breathwork

A simple breath technique that's surprisingly powerful. The breath is a direct line into the body — it gets past the part of your mind that knows how to stay composed and goes straight to what's actually held there. Most people have never experienced anything like it.

Block Work

We use a padded block and vocalization to give the body somewhere to put what it's been holding. The anger you swallowed. The "no" you never said. The grief that never quite came out.

Here's what most people don't expect: what comes after the discharge isn't exhaustion. It's aliveness. A current running through the body that most of us have been sitting on for years without knowing it. Life force isn't something you generate — it's something that was always there, waiting for the armor to come off. When it moves again, people describe feeling more themselves than they have in a long time. More connected, more present, more awake to the world around them.

Relational Presence

We practice being seen. It sounds simple. For most people it's the hardest part.

About the Facilitators

Jesse Torgerson

Jesse Torgerson has 40 years of experience facilitating emotional healing and personal growth throughout the U.S. and Europe. She holds master's degrees in counseling psychology from Antioch University and in spiritual psychology from the School of Spiritual Psychology in Milwaukee. Jesse lives in New Paltz, NY and works with individuals and groups in New York, Philadelphia, and Los Angeles.

Eli Harrigan

Eli Harrigan is a certified breathwork practitioner, somatic coach, and group facilitator. He brings analytical clarity and creative intuition to his work. With a background in Western philosophy, video production, and photography, it wasn't until Eli discovered somatics that he realized he had been living primarily inside his head — and that there was another, much more rewarding way of being.
