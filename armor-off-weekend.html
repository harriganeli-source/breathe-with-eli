Armor Off

Weekend Workshop

Somatics • Breathwork • Teachings • Group Processing • Shadow Work

June 13th & 14th

Gain insight into your unconscious patterns & conditioning.

Feel what needs to be felt.

Leave with new clarity and a deeper sense of yourself.

Register Now

Most of us have patterns that took shape long before we were old enough to choose them. What we learned to do with feelings, with needs, with closeness and conflict, became so habitual over time that it stopped feeling like a strategy and started feeling like us.

This weekend goes underneath that.

Through breathwork, somatic practice, and teachings on the inner life, you'll begin to see what's actually been directing your choices and your relationships.

What makes a weekend like this different from solitary practice is the group itself. Other people have a way of bringing things up in us that simply don't surface when we're alone. In a safe, well-held container, that becomes the work. What moves between people in a room together turns out to be some of the most honest and revealing material available.

Most people walk out feeling lighter than when they came in, and more awake to what's actually possible for them.
