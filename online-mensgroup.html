Are there places in your life where you feel stuck?

Are you numbing out? Struggling with motivation or focus?

Do you feel disconnected from the people you love most?

Do you struggle to let go and be vulnerable with friends or loved ones?

Do you want to be a better man, father, and partner, but don't know how?

This is a space to bring your full self.

The men's group is a space for men to bring their full selves: their struggle, judgment, doubt, pain, and also—and perhaps most vulnerably—their joy, hope, and excitement.

Everything is invited.

Do you struggle to understand your own feelings? Do you find it hard to stay present during difficult conversations? Do you feel stuck in repeating patterns?

Most men were never given the tools to process their emotions. We were taught to push through, figure it out, stay strong. And while there's nothing wrong with strength, unprocessed emotions don't just disappear. They show up as anxiety, numbness, disconnection, anger, or a quiet sense that something is missing.

This group exists to change that. Here, we practice slowing down. We learn to feel what we actually feel—not what we think we should feel. We process the stuck places. We transform shame into connection. And we hold each other accountable to showing up for ourselves and the people we love.

Everything is invited. Nothing is too much. You don't need to have it figured out to begin.

About Eli

Eli is a men's group facilitator and certified breathwork practitioner who has studied with David Elliott in New Mexico and Jesse Torgerson in New York.

As a photographer and video editor—and with an education in Western philosophy—it wasn't until Eli discovered breathwork that he realized he had been living his life entirely inside his head, and that there was another, much more rewarding way of being. The work has been revolutionary in his personal and professional life, and he is passionate about sharing it with others.
