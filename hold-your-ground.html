Hold Your Ground

Nervous System Training for Men

Picture this: someone in your life goes sideways emotionally. A partner's voice rises. A kid hits the floor. A coworker comes in hot. And something in you wants to shut down, get defensive, or fix it fast just to make it stop.

That instinct makes sense. Your nervous system is doing what it learned to do. The problem is that it's running the show in moments when you'd rather be choosing.

The work in this workshop is about learning to stay. Not to perform calm, but to actually be grounded in your body while someone else isn't in theirs. That's harder than it sounds, and more learnable than most people think.

Over three to five two-hour sessions, a small group of men will work together using breathwork and somatic practice. We'll build the capacity to recognize what's happening in our own bodies, breathe through activation rather than react from it, and develop the kind of steady internal ground that makes us a reliable presence for the people we care about. This is in-person work. That matters.

What You'll Build

Most of us learned to handle emotional pressure by leaving, not physically, but internally. We go into our heads, get analytical, try to solve what can't be solved in that moment. What this training builds is the capacity to stay in contact with another person's experience without needing to change it, fix it, or escape it. That steadiness is not passive. It's one of the more demanding things a person can do.

When something activates your nervous system, whether it's a raised voice, a hard silence, or the particular tone that means this is serious, there's a physiological cascade that happens faster than thought. Learning to breathe through that moment, rather than react from it, changes the whole trajectory of what comes next. You'll develop a felt sense of your own activation and the skill to work with it in real time.

The people in your life don't need you to be unaffected. They need you to be present. There's a difference between someone who's shut down and someone who's grounded, and the people around you feel that difference immediately. This training builds the internal capacity to be the kind of presence that doesn't make a hard moment harder.

A lot of the damage in relationships and at work happens because we don't catch ourselves quickly enough. We're already reacting by the time we realize we've been activated. This work teaches you to recognize the signs earlier, the tightness in your chest, the shift in your breathing, the impulse to withdraw or push back, so you have more room to choose what happens next.

About Eli

I spent fifteen years living almost entirely in my head. I was a philosopher and a video editor. I was good at thinking about feelings. I was not good at having them.

That first time cracked something open. I shook, I cried, I beat up pillows. I felt completely different in my body and my mind afterward. And I started to see how much of my life I'd been living unconsciously, driven by old patterns I couldn't see.

Since then, this work has changed how I handle pressure, how I connect with people, and what I'm willing to commit to. It taught me how to be with fear and doubt instead of running from them. Without it, I don't think I would have gotten married, had a child, or had the guts to do this work at all.

I bring breathwork, somatic practice, and years studying with some of the most experienced practitioners in the field to this work. I've spent a lot of time in the seat myself, and I carry all of it into the room.
